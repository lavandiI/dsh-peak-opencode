# OpenCode Go cost/peak

The band above the composer shows the current model's tariff and its price per 1M tokens.

- **Peak** — pink, with the time left until the period ends.
- **Off-peak** — green, also with a countdown.
- **Flat** — blue: the model has no peak or off-peak hours.
- **Free** — green: all tokens cost $0, and there is no period.

The left side shows the price of the current tariff (input · output · cache, plus cache write when the model bills it); the right side shows the phase, the period end in your time zone, and the time left.

Settings live on this page: model catalog, peak windows, behavior, custom CSS and reset. The plugin always follows the DSH interface language.
